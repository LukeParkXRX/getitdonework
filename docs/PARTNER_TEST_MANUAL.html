Quick Test Guide — for our U.S. Partner

A 5-minute walkthrough of exactly what the experts you invite will experience.

What we'd love you to do Sign up as an expert yourself, go through the steps below, and tell us if anything feels confusing or broken. This is the same flow your invited experts will use.
Expert application link
https://getitdonework.com/enabler-apply

1Apply as an expert (~3 min)

  1. Open the link above. You should see the Enabler application form.
  2. Submit your name, email, school, location, expertise, and bio.
  3. Check that you receive an application confirmation email.
  4. After admin approval, open the private sign-up link from Get It Done at Work and create your account.
    If it's not in your inbox, check Spam and click "Not spam" — this helps for everyone.
  5. You're now signed in to your Enabler dashboard — everything is in English.

2Complete your profile (~2 min)

  1. Click Edit profile: add a photo, a short bio, your education, specialties, and your hourly rate. Click Save.
  2. Click Set availability: turn on a few days and add time ranges (e.g., Mon 09:00–12:00) in your own timezone. Click Save.
    This is important — startups can only book the times you set here.

3What happens next

  1. Your account starts as "Pending approval" — this is normal. Email us and our team approves you (usually the same day).
  2. Once approved, you appear in the expert directory and Korean startups can request sessions with you. You'll get an email + dashboard notification for each request.

  • Was the whole experience in English and clear?
  • Anything confusing, broken, or missing?
  • How long did the whole thing take?
